
My dear Clara
	I have just this moment received yours of the 9th and send you as requested the slips sent me by sister.
	I wish you would go or send to Hamilton & Lovering's Photograph gallery to see if the plate of Churchill's photograph was not preserved. Usually the plates are kept. If this has been have more of the photographs taken. If not I will send you send you down the only remaining onewhich has not been retouched and you can have others taken from that.
	I called to see Nannie about 3 o'clock today (Sunday)  but she had not returned since going to church this morning.
	The weather is dark & gloomy, threatening storms, I am well, reasonably, have no appetite & can't sleep but these things seem to have no effect on me.

	Affectionately,
		APC

I wish you would send immediately to Mr. Jenkins and get me Mr. Faxer's address and send it to me. Mr Higgins [illeg] Mr. Jenkins will & can get the address for you. Don’t fail to attend to this at once.
	In our trouble I can give you no consolation. My heart is full of bitterness & curses. I want nothing more but vengeance on the murderers of our son.
